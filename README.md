# Internship at PT. PLN Nusantara Power UPDK Bandar Lampung

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains the results of my internship project at **PT. PLN Nusantara Power UPDK Bandar Lampung**. The project focuses on evaluating the impact of the Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) program **Peduli DAS Sungai V (PEDAS V)** using the **Social Return on Investment (SROI)** methodology.

This internship project was conducted by a team of three members. My primary responsibility was analyzing the **PEDAS V** CSR program, including data preparation, stakeholder analysis, impact evaluation, SROI calculation, and interpretation of the results. Other CSR programs were analyzed by my teammates, while the overall discussions and report preparation were completed collaboratively.

---

## 🌱 Background

PT. PLN Nusantara Power UPDK Bandar Lampung implements Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programs based on the principles of **ISO 26000**, demonstrating its commitment to sustainable development.

One of its flagship initiatives is the **Peduli Daerah Aliran Sungai (PEDAS)** program, which aims to rehabilitate critical land in the Way Besai Watershed through sustainable coffee farming practices. The program seeks to reduce soil erosion and sedimentation, improve environmental quality, enhance farmers' livelihoods, and support the long-term operation of the Way Besai Hydroelectric Power Plant (PLTA).

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Evaluate the social, economic, and environmental impacts of the PEDAS V CSR program.
- Measure the social value generated using the Social Return on Investment (SROI) framework.
- Assess the effectiveness of the CSR investment.
- Provide recommendations for future CSR program improvements.

---

## 🔍 Methodology

The project follows the Social Return on Investment (SROI) framework through the following stages:

- Problem Identification
- Data Collection (Primary & Secondary Data)
- Data Preparation
  - Data Cleaning
  - Data Transformation
- Stakeholder Identification
- Outcome Mapping
- Financial Proxy Assignment
- SROI Calculation
- Result Interpretation

---

## 📊 Results

The analysis produced an **SROI ratio of 0.36**, indicating that every **IDR 1** invested generated approximately **IDR 0.36** in measurable social value during the evaluation period.

Although the measurable return has not yet exceeded the investment value, the program has delivered several positive outcomes:

- Increased income for coffee farmer groups.
- Reduced farming operational costs.
- Lower fertilizer and pesticide expenses.
- Improved knowledge and agricultural skills through training.
- Adoption of sustainable farming practices.
- Positive contributions to environmental conservation and watershed management.

The results suggest that the program's long-term benefits are expected to increase as conservation efforts continue and coffee plantations become more productive.
